Methodology & limitations

Costs assume 8bps commission ($6 AUD minimum per order) and 10bps adverse slippage (~36bps round-trip) — a stated assumption, not a measurement; recalibrate against real paper-trading fills once they exist. Entries fill at next-bar-open; stop-loss/take-profit are detected against each bar's high/low range (stop wins a same-bar tie). The universe is a hand-curated, sector-diversified static list — it carries survivorship bias (no later-delisted names) and is reviewed manually on a quarterly cadence, not automated. This page is read-only: nothing here can place, cancel, or modify an order.
